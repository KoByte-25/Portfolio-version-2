Project Description

"ChannelSphere" is a multi-vendor order and delivery management platform built for businesses that sell and deliver physical products. It brings customers, companies, and delivery staff into one system so orders can be placed, assigned, tracked, and completed in a clear and organized way.

Customers:
must create an account before ordering. After logging in, they can browse available companies, choose the company they want to buy from, and place an order for the product they need. All active orders appear in the Order Items page. Customers can cancel an order only while it has not yet been loaded onto a delivery vehicle. Once the order has been put on a delivery vehicle and delivery has started, cancellation is no longer allowed. At that stage, customers can use the See Location feature to track the delivery vehicle in real time on a map. Completed orders move to the Delivered Orders page, where customers can search by date and use quick filters such as Today and All Time.

Distribution companies:
also create their own accounts to receive and manage orders. They can add delivery vehicles through a form, define vehicle capacity, and assign undelivered orders only to available vehicles. A vehicle can only receive orders while it is available and has free capacity. When the company is ready, it can click Deliver Orders to send the vehicle out. Once a vehicle starts delivering, it becomes unavailable for new assignments until it returns. Companies can monitor vehicle status, view on-delivery orders, track vehicle location live on a map, and review delivered orders on a dedicated Delivered page with date search and quick filters such as Today and All Time.

Vehicle drivers and delivery staff:
can see assigned orders only after the company has sent the vehicle out for delivery. They can mark each order as Delivered after completing the drop-off. When no more orders remain, the driver sees an I’ve Returned button to confirm that the vehicle has come back to the company. This makes the vehicle available again for new orders.
